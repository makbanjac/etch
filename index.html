Install

  1. Open Terminal (press ⌘-Space, type "Terminal", press Return).
  2. Paste this line and press Return:
curl -fsSL https://etchapp.cc/install.sh | bash

A few seconds later the glass is on your desktop, and Etch is in your Dock for settings. macOS 13 or newer, Apple Silicon or Intel. Read the installer before you run it. It is short.

Unlock Pro: buy a key, open Etch, paste the key under Licence and click Activate.

Questions

Why Terminal, and not a download?A downloaded app must be approved by Apple to open without warnings. The one-line install skips that step and shows no warnings. The script only puts two apps in your Applications folder and starts the widget.

What does it collect?Nothing. Etch has no analytics and no account. It goes online once, to check a Pro key with the shop when you activate it.

How do I remove it?Run curl -fsSL https://etchapp.cc/uninstall.sh | bash. It moves Etch to the Trash.

Updates?Run the install line again. Your text, positions and licence stay.
